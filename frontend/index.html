The Journey of Every Batch

Every agricultural batch undergoes verified milestone entries submitted by authorized participants across the supply chain.

1

Farmer / Origin

Initial harvest recorded with farm GPS coordinates, crop variety, planting date, and initial quantity.

2

Collection Center

Intake moisture testing, visual grading, batch acceptance, and standardized container packaging.

3

Logistics & Storage

Custody transfer handoffs with vehicle numbers, transit routes, and temperature-controlled warehousing.

4

Processing & Lab

Industrial processing, mass-conservation tracking, and accredited quality compliance certificates.

5

Retail Distribution

Retail SKU listing, supermarket inventory intake, and point-of-sale customer availability.

6

Consumer Trust

Instant QR code scan revealing complete provenance history, certifier signatures, and transaction IDs.
